# Immune-Onc Therapeutics

> Immune-Onc Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Palo Alto, California, founded in 2016 by Charlene Liao and Guo Liang Yu. It develops novel antibody therapeutics targeting myeloid cell inhibitory receptors — principally the Leukocyte Immunoglobulin-Like Receptor subfamily B (LILRB) — for blood cancers, solid tumors, and autoimmune and inflammatory disease. Immune-Onc is a therapeutics developer, not a software or platform company. It publishes NO public API, no developer portal, no OpenAPI or other machine-readable contract, no SDKs, and no /.well-known/ discovery surface. The links below are the company's real published web properties and its public clinical-trial registry entries.

## Pipeline

- [Pipeline overview](https://www.immune-onc.com/pipeline): Full development pipeline and stage of each program.
- [IO-108](https://www.immune-onc.com/io108): Fully human IgG4 antagonist antibody targeting LILRB2 (ILT4); Phase 1b/2 in solid tumors including hepatocellular carcinoma.
- [IO-202](https://www.immune-onc.com/io-202): First-in-class IgG1 antibody targeting LILRB4 (ILT3); Phase 1b in AML and CMML; FDA Fast Track and Orphan Drug designations.
- [IO-106](https://www.immune-onc.com/io106): LAIR1-targeting myeloid and stromal checkpoint inhibitor candidate.
- [Blood cancers](https://www.immune-onc.com/blood-cancers): Hematologic malignancy programs.
- [Solid tumors](https://www.immune-onc.com/solid-tumors): Solid tumor programs.
- [Immunology and inflammation](https://www.immune-onc.com/immunology-inflammation): Autoimmune and inflammatory disease programs.
